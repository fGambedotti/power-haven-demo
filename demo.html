Baseline vs VoltPilot

Model basis

Historic data, static demo, transparent assumptions.

The demo is an annualised representative simulation. It compares a baseline datacentre that keeps its UPS battery reserved for backup with a VoltPilot case that preserves the reserve margin, charges during lower-price and lower-carbon renewable-surplus hours, and discharges during higher-price peak hours.

Data layer

Representative 2024-2025 GB market profiles for price, carbon intensity, renewable share, fossil share, demand stress and curtailment signal.

Site layer

Selected site load, battery MW/MWh, reserve percentage, round-trip efficiency and operating mode define available flexibility.

Dispatch layer

VoltPilot charges in cleaner/cheaper windows, discharges in peak windows, and never uses the protected backup reserve.

Important caveat

This is not a quotation or site audit. A real assessment would use half-hourly meter data, contracted tariffs and audited market feeds.
